MINING RULES

Rules, locked in

These numbers are already decided and baked into the contracts. Nothing here can be changed after deploy.

Mining rules (already decided)

  • Token $AID ("Agent ID", 18 decimals), max supply 21,000,000. Only the miner contract may mint, and never above the cap.
  • Mining is only for wallets holding a Binance Agent ID (contract 0x60B9262456dE011F7d15a54CdD8c48bAaa8B022F, checked via balanceOf). Sessions are Free (3 hours, x1, 0 BNB, repeatable) or Paid (12 hours, x3, 0.003 BNB), picked in startMining(bool paid); accrual per second, claim() mints the accrued $AID.
  • 2 machine slots per wallet. Each machine earns 100 $AID/day in epoch 1 (full rig = 200/day). 4 epochs @ 5,250,000 $AID: rate x1, x0.5, x0.25, x0.125. Once 21M is mined, mining stops.
  • Session fees: Free sessions cost 0 BNB; Paid sessions cost exactly 0.003 BNB. Free is 3 hours at x1, repeatable as often as you like. Paid is 12 hours at x3. A wrong amount reverts (no refund). The fee is split: 90% (0.0027 BNB) goes straight to the treasury (owner wallet, set in Step 4, immutable) and 10% (0.0003 BNB) to the referrer if that wallet set a referrer before the session started — with no referrer, 100% goes to the treasury. The referrer share is held in the contract (pull) and withdrawn by the referrer via claimReferralFees(); no BNB is ever stuck without a destination.
  • Presale: 2,100,000 $AID (10% of supply) at 1 BNB = 210,000 $AID, hard cap 10 BNB. Min buy 0.01 BNB, max 1 BNB per wallet (cumulative), sale lasts 7 days or until sold out. Open to anyone — no Agent ID needed. Buyers pay BNB to the presale contract; the BNB stays there until the sale ends, then the owner withdraws it to the treasury. $AID is sent to the buyer immediately on buy. The presale allocation is minted to the presale contract by the miner (Step 6, once only) and counts toward totalMinted, so Epoch 1 keeps 3,150,000 $AID for miners (5,250,000 − 2,100,000). Mining supply after the presale: 18,900,000 $AID.
  • Machine upgrades: Turbo (x2) = 500 $AID + 0.003 BNB, Quantum (x4) = 2,000 $AID + 0.003 BNB. The $AID cost is burned — no $AID piles up in the miner contract, so no withdraw function is needed — and the 0.003 BNB upgrade fee is split 90% treasury / 10% referrer, like a paid session fee.
  • Referral: setReferrer() once per wallet. When the referee claims, the referrer gets +10% minted on top — never taken from the referee.
  • The web (artifact agent-id-miner) must NOT be connected to these contracts yet — wiring to the real contracts happens after this deploy is done and the addresses are recorded.
DEPLOY STEPS

Deploy in order

Work through the steps top to bottom. Each step fills in the next one automatically.

1. Network & Wallet

Open this page in your wallet's dApp browser (OKX first, then MetaMask / Trust / Binance Wallet). The wallet that deploys will become the owner of the $AID token.

Not connected yet.

2. Deploy $AID Token

Constructor: owner_ = deployer wallet (automatic from the connected wallet). Deploy this first — the token address is needed in Steps 3 and 4.

3. Deploy Presale Contract

Constructor: token_ = $AID address from Step 2, owner_ = deployer wallet (automatic). The presale sells 2,100,000 $AID at 1 BNB = 210,000 $AID (hard cap 10 BNB, min 0.01 BNB, max 1 BNB per wallet, 7 days). Buyers pay BNB to this contract and the BNB stays here until the sale ends — only then can you withdraw it. Deploy this before the miner (Step 4), because the miner needs the presale address in its constructor.

4. Deploy Miner Contract

Constructor: token_ = $AID address from Step 2, agentId_ = Binance Agent ID contract (pre-filled, cannot be changed), treasury_ = wallet that receives 90% of paid fees — sessions and upgrades (and 100% when there is no referrer), presale_ = presale address from Step 3. The treasury cannot be changed after deploy — double-check the address. It defaults to the deployer wallet; change it only if you want fees to go to a different wallet.

5. Grant Mint Rights to the Miner (once only!)

Call setMinter(miner) on the $AID token from the owner wallet. After this, only the miner contract can mint $AID and this right can never be moved again.

6. Fund the Presale (once only!)

Call allocatePresale() on the miner contract. This mints the presale allocation — 2,100,000 $AID — to the presale contract. Anyone can call it, once only, and only after Step 5 made the miner the minter. After this, the presale holds the full allocation and Epoch 1 keeps 3,150,000 $AID for miners.

7. Check Contracts After Deploy

Fill in all three addresses (or leave them auto-filled), then check. All values must match before continuing.

8. Start the Presale (7 days)

Call startSale() on the presale contract. This starts the 7-day sale (or until 2,100,000 $AID sells out). It only works after Step 6 funded the presale. Once started, anyone can buy $AID at 1 BNB = 210,000 $AID (min 0.01 BNB, max 1 BNB per wallet) and the $AID is sent to the buyer immediately. The BNB stays in the presale contract until the sale ends — withdraw it to the treasury afterwards with withdrawBNB(), and reclaim any unsold $AID with reclaimUnsold().

FAQ

Before you deploy

Short answers to the questions that matter on mainnet.

What does this page do?

It deploys the three $AID contracts — token, presale and miner — on BNB Mainnet, grants mint rights to the miner, funds the presale, and checks every value on-chain. It is an owner-only tool.

Why mainnet only?

There is no testnet step. Every deploy here is on BNB Mainnet (chain 56), cannot be undone, and costs real BNB gas. Double-check the treasury address in Step 4.

Where do session fees go?

90% (0.0027 BNB) of every paid fee — Paid sessions and upgrades — goes straight to the treasury wallet from Step 4, and 10% (0.0003 BNB) to the referrer. Free sessions pay nothing. The treasury is set once in the constructor and cannot be changed.

How does the presale work?

2,100,000 $AID at 1 BNB = 210,000 $AID, hard cap 10 BNB, 7 days. Buyers pay BNB to the presale contract; the BNB stays there until the sale ends, then the owner withdraws it.

Will this page ask for my seed phrase?

Never. Every transaction is signed in your wallet. Never share your seed phrase or private key with anyone, including a marketplace, an agent, or a buyer.
