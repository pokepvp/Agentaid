How to play (simulation)

  1. Connect your Agent ID — one tap in the console creates a simulated identity. No wallet needed.
  2. Start a machine — pick Free or Paid — each of your 2 slots runs one session per start. Free lasts 3 hours, costs 0 BNB, and mines at ×1 speed (100 $AID/day rate in Epoch 1, accruing only during those 3 hours). Paid lasts 12 hours, costs 0.003 BNB from your simulated BNB wallet charged instantly at start, and mines at ×3 speed (300 $AID/day rate in Epoch 1, ≈150 $AID per full Paid session at base tier). Paid stacks with upgrades: Turbo ×2 + Paid ×3 = ×6, Quantum ×4 + Paid ×3 = ×12. If your simulated BNB is below 0.003, the Paid start is refused — top up or start Free instead.
  3. Claim $AID — machines accrue every second. Claim whenever you like, per slot or all at once.
  4. Upgrade — each upgrade costs mined $AID plus 0.003 BNB from your simulated BNB wallet: Turbo Miner ×2 = 500 $AID + 0.003 BNB, Quantum Rig ×4 = 2,000 $AID + 0.003 BNB. Unclaimed $AID on your machines counts too — tapping Upgrade claims it for you first. If your BNB balance is below 0.003, the upgrade is refused — tap Top Up first.
  5. Invite — share your referral code and earn +10% of what invitees mine, minted on top, plus 10% of their session fees.

Progress is saved in this browser only. The numbers match the real contract's design, so what you learn here carries over when mining opens.

Is $AID real yet?

No. The contracts are written and tested, but they are not deployed — there is no contract address, no liquidity, and no market price. Everything on this page today is a simulation with play balances. If someone offers you "$AID" now, it is not this token.

Where do session fees go?

Only Paid sessions have a fee — Free sessions cost 0 BNB and charge nothing. On the real contract design, each Paid session's 0.003 BNB fee, paid up front for a 12-hour session at ×3 speed, is split automatically: 90% (0.0027 BNB) goes to the project treasury wallet set at deployment, and 10% (0.0003 BNB) goes to the referrer of the miner, who claims it themselves. The miner contract has no owner who can redirect fees. In this simulation the same split is shown with play BNB.

What is an Agent ID?

A Binance Agent ID is an on-chain identity for AI agents on BNB Chain. The real AgentIdMiner contract checks that your wallet holds one before it lets you start a mining session — your agent's identity is its mining license. In the simulation, the "Connect" button in the console stands in for that check.

Can I lose my progress?

In the simulation, yes — progress lives only in this browser's local storage, so clearing site data resets the game. Once mining is live on-chain, balances and sessions will live in the contracts instead.

Whitepaper — summary

The short version, on one page. A full PDF whitepaper is not published yet — this summary is the current canonical text.

01 · PROBLEM

Agents have no way to earn

AI agents can hold wallets and call contracts, but almost nothing is built for them to do productively on-chain. At the same time, token launches usually hand supply to insiders first and let the public buy later. $AID flips that: aside from a single public presale of 2,100,000 $AID (10% of supply, taken from Epoch 1), the supply enters the world only through an agent identity doing the work of mining, one session at a time (Free 3h, Paid 12h), under rules anyone can read in the contract.

02 · MINING ECONOMY

Work in sessions, earn by the second

A miner starts a session — Free for 3 hours, or Paid for 12 hours — and accrues $AID every second at the current epoch rate. A Free Miner earns 100 $AID per day in Epoch 1. Two machine slots per miner keep the game balanced, and upgrades multiply output: Turbo Miner ×2 and Quantum Rig ×4. Every session is a choice: start Free at ×1 speed for 0 BNB (the first session is free on this path), or pay 0.003 BNB up front for a Paid session at ×3 speed — the paid multiplier stacks with tier upgrades, so a Quantum Rig on a Paid session runs at ×12. A full Free session (3h at the 100 $AID/day rate) earns ≈12.5 $AID at base tier in Epoch 1; a full Paid session (12h at the 300 $AID/day rate) earns ≈150 $AID. Free sessions keep mining open to anyone; the paid option only buys speed and time.

03 · TOKENOMICS

21 million — 10% presale, 90% mined

Max supply is 21,000,000 $AID (18 decimals, BNB Chain). Of that, 2,100,000 $AID (10%) is the presale allocation, taken from Epoch 1; the remaining 18,900,000 $AID (90%) is emitted through mining. Mining emission runs in 4 epochs: Epoch 1 contributes 3,150,000 $AID of mining on top of the presale allocation, and Epochs 2–4 contribute 5,250,000 $AID each. The reward rate halves each epoch: ×1, ×0.5, ×0.25, ×0.125. There is no team or investor allocation in the design — beyond the public presale, $AID is created only when a miner's session accrues it. Upgrade payments (500 / 2,000 $AID) are burned, so active play permanently shrinks the circulating supply.

04 · REFERRAL

+10%, minted on top

Growth is rewarded without taxing the new miner. When an invitee mines, the referrer receives a bonus equal to 10% of the mined amount, minted on top — the invitee keeps 100%. Referrers also receive 10% of the session fees their invitees pay, claimable by the referrer directly from the contract, while the other 90% of each fee goes to the treasury.

05 · RISKS

Read this before you mine

$AID is not deployed yet: there is no contract address, no liquidity, and no price, and the launch timeline is undecided (all dates TBD). When it does launch, mined $AID will have value only if a market forms — it may trade at or near zero, and session fees paid in BNB are spent whether or not the token gains value. Smart contracts can contain bugs even after testing; no audit is claimed at this stage. The simulation on this page uses play balances and carries no value at all. Never pay anyone for "$AID" before the official contract address is published here.
