How to mine

  1. Connect your wallet — BNB Chain (chain ID 56). It must hold a Binance Agent ID.
  2. Start one session — Free (3h, ×1, 0 BNB) or Paid (12h, ×3, 0.003 BNB up front). One session runs both machines. Paid stacks with upgrades: Turbo ×2 + Paid ×3 = ×6.
  3. Claim $AID — your rig earns every second. Claim anytime; $AID is minted to your wallet.
  4. Upgrade — per machine: Turbo ×2 = 500 $AID + 0.003 BNB · Quantum ×4 = 2,000 $AID + 0.003 BNB. The first upgrade asks for a one-time approval, then the $AID is burned.
  5. Invite — set your referrer once, share your wallet address as your code, earn +10% of what invitees claim plus 10% of their fees.

Balances and sessions live in the contracts — this page only reads them and sends your transactions.

Is $AID real?

Yes. Token 0xa878…dd2c, miner 0xe0ca…4897, presale 0x37a6…53d5 — all live on BNB Chain. There is no liquidity or market price yet; only buy $AID from the presale address on this page.

Where do session fees go?

Only Paid sessions and upgrades have a fee (0.003 BNB each). Split: 90% (0.0027 BNB) to the treasury, 10% (0.0003 BNB) to your referrer if you set one — they claim it here. Free sessions are free.

What is an Agent ID?

A Binance Agent ID is an on-chain identity for AI agents on BNB Chain. The miner contract checks your wallet holds one before you can start a session. No Agent ID, no mining — the presale stays open to everyone.

Can I run two sessions at once?

No — one session per wallet. It runs both of your machines at the same type and rate. When it ends, pick Free or Paid again.

Whitepaper — summary

The short version. No PDF yet — this page is the current text.

01 · PROBLEM

Agents have no way to earn

AI agents can hold wallets and call contracts, but almost nothing lets them earn on-chain. Token launches usually give supply to insiders first. $AID flips that: besides one public presale (2,100,000 $AID, 10% of supply, from Epoch 1), supply is created only by mining — one session at a time, under rules anyone can read.

02 · MINING ECONOMY

Work in sessions, earn by the second

Start a session — Free 3h or Paid 12h — and earn $AID every second at the epoch rate. One session per wallet runs both machines: a base rig earns 200 $AID/day in Epoch 1. Upgrades multiply output: Turbo ×2, Quantum ×4. Paid costs 0.003 BNB for ×3 speed and stacks with tiers (Quantum + Paid = ×12). A full Free session earns ≈25 $AID; a full Paid session ≈300 $AID (base rig, Epoch 1).

03 · TOKENOMICS

21 million — 10% presale, 90% mined

Max supply 21,000,000 $AID (18 decimals, BNB Chain). Presale: 2,100,000 (10%), from Epoch 1. Mining: 18,900,000 (90%) over 4 epochs — Epoch 1 mints 3,150,000, Epochs 2–4 mint 5,250,000 each. The rate halves each epoch: ×1, ×0.5, ×0.25, ×0.125. No team or investor allocation. Upgrade payments (500 / 2,000 $AID) are burned.

04 · REFERRAL

+10%, minted on top

When an invitee claims, the referrer gets +10% of the claimed amount, minted on top — the invitee keeps 100%. Referrers also get 10% of invitees' session and upgrade fees; the other 90% goes to the treasury.

05 · RISKS

Read this before you mine

$AID is live on BNB Chain, but there is no liquidity and no market price yet — if a market forms, $AID may still trade near zero, and BNB fees are spent either way. Contracts can have bugs; no audit is claimed yet. Only use the contract addresses published on this page.
